Object consist of unitptr_t sized words.

tag in low 2 bits:
00 - object
10 - symbol
x1 - fixnum (masked to 31bits even on 64b platforms)

special values:
0x00000000 - LTE_INVALID
0xfffffffc - LTE_NIL

object layout:
next pointer (LSB is mark bit)
class pointer
... slots

symbol layout:
package pointer
symbol name

Symbols and packages are allocated outside of garbage collected heap and 
uncollectable.

Primitives and special forms follow the GC'd object layout, but live outside
of GC'd heap. These are not marked by collector and cannot contain references
to GC'd heap.
