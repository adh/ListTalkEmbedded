the VM heap is contiguos span of memory. each allocation is 16b aligned.
user application is responsible for providing appropriately sized and aligned
buffer.

The heap consits of allocated object of size determined by their class and
free space identified by having all zero header class word, next word is size
of the hole in 16B allocation units (two additional words are reserved for 
allocator).

Sweep phase iterates through the entire heap and deallocates non-marked 
objects, by marking them as free space. In the same scan it also coallesces
adjacent free space records and updates the freelist links accordingly.
